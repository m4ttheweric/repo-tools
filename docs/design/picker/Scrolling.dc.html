Height cap & scrolling

The viewport is min( caller cap, list length, pane height − chrome ). The cap defaults to 14 and is a per-call knob — a content-heavy command may raise it, a submenu may lower it — but the pane is always the hard ceiling: ask for 40 rows in a 20-row pane and you get what fits, scrolling. A short list still collapses to its content. Beyond the viewport the list scrolls inside the card: a 1-column thumb rail on the right edge (Panel, no track), and the header count is always the truth. The cursor scrolls with a 2-row margin (scrolloff) so context is never lost.
