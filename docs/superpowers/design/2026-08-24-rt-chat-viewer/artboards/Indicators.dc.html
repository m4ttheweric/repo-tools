Indicators Every marker the viewer shows, and the question each one answers. All of them are subordinate to the daemon banner.
listening
Signed in, tail armed and touching — will hear you now The tail heartbeat is fresh within 10 minutes, the arm itself counting as its first beat. A DM or mention becomes a notification in its pane.
idle
Signed on, not listening No tail armed; the session heartbeat is fresh within an hour. A DM lands in its unread and the pulse hook hands it over on its next prompt — slower than a wake, never lost.
deaf
Its tail died and nothing restarted it The one failure the CLI cannot prevent — the daemon went away, the session ended, or leave was called. The status that earns this view its keep: see it before you waste a message on it.
